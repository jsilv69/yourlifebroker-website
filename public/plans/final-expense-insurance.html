Final expense insurance for peace of mind

Final expense insurance — also called burial insurance or funeral insurance — is a small whole life policy designed to cover end-of-life costs like the funeral, casket, burial or cremation, and any lingering medical bills. The average funeral now costs thousands of dollars, and this coverage makes sure that bill never falls on your loved ones.

How final expense insurance works

You choose a modest coverage amount — typically between $5,000 and $25,000 — and pay a fixed, affordable premium for life. Because the benefit is smaller, qualifying is simple: most plans ask only a few health questions, and guaranteed-acceptance options are available with no medical exam and no health questions at all.

Who final expense insurance is right for

  • Seniors who want simple, affordable coverage for final costs
  • Anyone who has been declined for other life insurance
  • People who want to protect family from funeral debt
  • Those who want a fixed premium that never increases with age

Frequently Asked Questions

Final Expense Insurance questions, answered

What does final expense insurance cover?

The tax-free benefit can be used for anything, but it's designed to cover funeral and burial or cremation costs, the casket or urn, the service, and any remaining medical or personal bills.

Can I be turned down for final expense insurance?

With guaranteed-acceptance final expense plans, there are no medical exams and no health questions — you cannot be turned down for health reasons. Your agent will tell you which option fits you best.

Is there a waiting period?

Some guaranteed-acceptance plans include a short waiting period for natural causes (accidental death is usually covered immediately). Plans with a few health questions often pay the full benefit from day one.

How much final expense coverage do I need?

Most families choose between $10,000 and $20,000, which covers a typical funeral, burial, and remaining bills. Your agent can help you estimate the right amount.
