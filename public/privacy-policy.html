Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YourLifeBroker ("YourLifeBroker," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit yourlifebroker.com (the "Site") or request a quote from us. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide. When you complete a quote form or contact us, we collect the details you submit — such as your name, email address, phone number, age, gender, state, nicotine use, coverage interests, and any other information you choose to share.

Information collected automatically. When you visit the Site, we and our service providers automatically collect certain information, including your IP address, device and browser type, operating system, referring pages, the pages you view, and how you interact with the Site. We collect this information using cookies and similar technologies described below.

2. Cookies, Pixels & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, web beacons, and tracking pixels to operate the Site, understand how it is used, and measure and improve our advertising. Some of these technologies are provided by third-party platforms, including:

  • Meta (Facebook / Instagram) — the Meta Pixel, used to measure ad performance and deliver relevant ads across Meta's platforms.
  • Google — Google Tag Manager, Google Ads, and related analytics and conversion tags, used to measure traffic, conversions, and advertising performance.
  • Roku — the Roku advertising pixel, used to measure and attribute advertising performance.

These technologies may collect information such as your device identifiers, IP address, and browsing activity, may recognize your device across different sites and services, and may allow these platforms to show you relevant advertising. We may also receive URL parameters (such as ad click identifiers and campaign tags) that help us understand how you found us.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can opt out of interest-based advertising through tools such as the Digital Advertising Alliance and the Network Advertising Initiative, as well as directly in your Meta and Google ad settings. Disabling cookies may affect how the Site functions.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to your quote requests and connect you with a licensed insurance agent.
  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Site and our services.
  • Communicate with you by phone, text, or email about your request and our services.
  • Measure, attribute, and optimize our marketing and advertising.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, and comply with our legal obligations.

4. How We Share Your Information

We may share your information with:

  • Licensed insurance agents and carriers, to prepare quotes and service your request.
  • Service providers that help us operate the Site and our business — such as hosting, CRM, analytics, and advertising platforms (including those listed above).
  • Legal and safety recipients, when required by law or to protect our rights.
  • Business transfers, in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

We do not sell your personal information for money. However, our use of advertising pixels may be considered "sharing" or a "sale" of information under some state privacy laws. You may opt out as described above.

5. Your Choices & Rights

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time — for example, by replying STOP to text messages or using the unsubscribe link in our emails. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to opt out of certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

6. Data Retention & Security

We retain personal information for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes described in this policy and to meet our legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Third-Party Links

The Site may link to third-party websites and services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties.

8. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted, and the "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hello@yourlifebroker.com or (810) 512-7397.
